# No Man's Sky Recipes: Assessment B (detector and browser evidence)

**Setup**
- Date: 2026-10-18.
- Target: https://nomansskyrecipes.com, live, plus the source at `nms-design` (`design/impeccable`).
- Tools: Impeccable 4.6.0 (CLI 0.1.15), run isolated from Assessment A; headless Playwright Chromium.
- Raw output: `game-sites/_output/design/nms/detector/`:
  - `cli/`, `url/` and `probes/`: scan and probe results;
  - `shots/`: screenshots;
  - `scripts/`: the scripts that produced them.

**Scope**
- CLI scan of `src/`: 89 files, **6 findings**.
- URL scans of 11 page types at 1440 and 390: **226 findings**, including 45 errors from the NitroPay ad scripts.
- The overlay results matched the URL scans one for one.
- Of the deduplicated findings, 13 are true positives, 3 are weak and 6 are false positives.

## Ranked true positives
1. **`/alliances` overflows on phones (high).**
   - The card grid (`alliances/index.astro:145`) has no base column setting.
   - One summary contains an unbroken fandom URL ("The Colonial Fleet of Kobol"), which stretches the grid to 401px wide.
   - Result: the page lays out at 429px and phones render it zoomed out. It's the only page affected.
   - Fix: `grid-cols-1` and `overflow-wrap:anywhere` on `.card .summary`.
2. **Alliance tag contrast (high, accessibility).** The alliance's own colour is used as text on `#05131c`. 10 colours fail, from black at 1.12:1 (6 tags are invisible) up to 4.01:1. Fix: clamp each colour to 4.5:1, as `readableTextOn` does for game colours, or put the tag on a swatch.
3. **Every bold is faux bold (high, craft).**
   - Only FuturaProBook 400 ships (`main.css:69-76`).
   - Per page, 15-65% of the text renders at weight 500/600/700, which the browser fakes.
   - Fix: ship real Medium/Demi/Bold faces (or a variable font), or limit weights to 400 and set `font-synthesis: none`.
4. **Templated hero on home and `/creatures` (medium, an AI-look tell).** A purple-to-slate gradient band, a wide-tracked "Refiners | Crafting | Cooking | And more..." strap and a centred title. It's the only purple on the site.
5. **An ad slot comes first after the hero or header (medium).**
   - Affected pages: home, products, calculator, items, refining and cooking.
   - On no-fill or ad-blocked visits, the first screen shows a 250px gap, from `NitroAd.astro:31`.
   - Fix: keep the height reservation (it's why layout shift is zero), but collapse it on no-fill or move it below the first content block.
6. **The item page's main icon is lazy-loaded (medium, performance).** `ANTIMATTER.webp` is above the fold, so mobile LCP lands about 400 ms after FCP (856 vs 1252 ms). Fix: `loading="eager"` and `fetchpriority="high"` in the item template, which fixes about 4,800 pages.
7. **Blog post reading (medium).**
   - Lines run to a median 76 and a maximum of 100 characters per line at 1440.
   - Headings have equal space above and below.
   - Fix: cap prose at about 68ch, more space above headings and less below.
8. **A separate style on alliances, feedback and the power calculator (medium/low).**
   - Tracked-caps `.eyebrow` labels (9 uses).
   - Rounded cards with a coloured top cap (`alliances.css:102`).
   - A side-border header (`alliances/[slug].astro:57`).
   - A hand-rolled spacing scale: 35 distinct values versus 7-15 elsewhere.
9. **The active page button in Tabulator** is black on `#1447e6`, 3.07:1 (refining, cooking). Use white text.
10. **Minor:**
    - Intro lines on `/items` (126 cpl) and `/products` (90 cpl).
    - The footer disclaimer has 1.2 line height.
    - Home and creatures body text has 0.05em letter-spacing.

**False positives:**
- Tabulator's sort triangles.
- The crafting-tree guide line.
- The mood colours on companions.
- Tabulator cells flagged as cards.
- The NitroPay script errors (from the headless environment).

**Weak:**
- The tagline tracking (it's display size).
- The cyan breadcrumb (only flagged next to the purple).
- The item chip padding (0.2px under the threshold).

## Probes
- **Contrast:** clean everywhere except #2 and #9. The game-colour badges pass.
- **Target size:** passes WCAG 2.5.8 AA on every page.
  - The floating search button covers card buttons at 1440 (check 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured).
  - 29-93 controls per page are under the 44px AAA size, mostly sidebar links.
- **Layout shift:** 0 on 20 of 22 runs; the maximum was 0.016.
- **Spacing:** on the 4.5px grid almost everywhere except alliances (50%).
- **Performance:** median of 3 runs; mobile is DPR 2, 4x CPU and 150 ms / 1.6 Mbps.

  | Page | Desktop LCP | Mobile LCP |
  |---|---|---|
  | Home | 124 ms | 1232 ms |
  | Item | 116 ms | 1252 ms |

  - Other numbers: TTFB about 45 ms, first-party CSS 15 KB, font 19 KB.
  - NitroPay adds about 437 KB per page, which is more than all the first-party bytes on mobile.
  - Item icons are 256px but shown at 24-48px. A 64/96px `srcset` would cut about 75%.
  - The logo is 2.7x oversized.

## Limitations
- Ads never rendered headless, so ad-fill layout shift and ad weight were not measured.
- These are lab numbers, not field data.
