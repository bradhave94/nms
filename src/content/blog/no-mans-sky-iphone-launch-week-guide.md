No Man's Sky reaches iPhone and iPad this week. Here's what the App Store listing now confirms, what's still unannounced, and a short plan for your first few hours with the exact recipe amounts.

Device requirements, settlement limits and controls are in our [iOS guide from announcement day](/blog/no-mans-sky-ios-guide/). This page covers what's new since then and what to do once you've landed.

## Launch facts, checked

I read the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/no-mans-sky/id1615755735) and Hello Games' [iOS announcement](https://www.nomanssky.com/2026/10/no-mans-sky-ios/) on October 25.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Release | October 28 (Hello Games). The App Store shows October 29 |
| Download | Free, 3.8 GB |
| Full game | One-time unlock: $19.99 / £19.99 |
| Free part | "The beginning of the game" |
| System | iOS or iPadOS 26.0 or later |
| Multiplayer | Not supported |
| Cross-save | Not announced |

### October 28 or 29?

Hello Games' post and its Deep Dive video both say October 28. The App Store pages for the US and the UK both say "Expected Oct 29, 2026" when I checked. It may come down to time zones, or the date may move. Either way, if you pre-order, Apple's [App Store feature](https://apps.apple.com/us/iphone/story/id6815432329) says it downloads automatically on release.

### What the $19.99 unlock covers

The App Store lists a single in-app purchase, Full Game Unlock. The listing says one purchase unlocks the full game with "10 years of updates". Hello Games says it's the full universe with over forty major updates, including [Cosmos](/blog/nms-cosmos-update/) and Voyagers.

Two things nobody has said yet: exactly where the free part stops, and whether future updates are included in the unlock.

### Cross-save and cross-play

Hello Games' [cross-save page](https://cloud.nomanssky.com/cross-save) lists PC, PlayStation, Xbox, Switch and Mac. iOS isn't on it, and the iOS announcement doesn't mention cross-save at all.

There's no cross-play either, because multiplayer isn't supported on iOS. If you already play on another platform, expect to start a new save on your phone for now.

## Before you hit Get

- **Update to iOS 26.** The store requires iOS or iPadOS 26.0 or later.
- **Free up space.** The store lists 3.8 GB. Leave some extra room for updates.
- **Pair a controller if you have one.** Touch controls were rebuilt for iOS, but controllers are supported too.

## Your first hours

Everything below comes from the game data this site is built on. If you're new, the [first-crafts table in our iOS guide](/blog/no-mans-sky-ios-guide/#first-crafts-for-new-ios-travellers) covers Metal Plating, Life Support Gel and launch fuel. This picks up from there.

### Portable Refiner recipes

The [Portable Refiner](/technology/BUILD_REFINER1/) (1 Metal Plating + 30 Oxygen) takes one ingredient at a time. These are the one-ingredient recipes you'll use most.

| Put in | Get out |
|---|---|
| 1 Ferrite Dust | 1 [Pure Ferrite](/raw/LAND2/) |
| 2 Pure Ferrite | 1 [Magnetised Ferrite](/raw/LAND3/) |
| 2 Carbon | 1 [Condensed Carbon](/raw/FUEL2/) |
| 2 Sodium | 1 [Sodium Nitrate](/raw/CATALYST2/) |
| 30 Di-hydrogen | 1 [Di-hydrogen Jelly](/products/JELLY/) |
| 2 Copper | 1 [Chromatic Metal](/raw/STELLAR2/) |
| 5 Tritium | 1 Di-hydrogen |
| 1 [Kelp Sac](/raw/PLANT_WATER/) | 1 Oxygen |
| 1 [Marrow Bulb](/raw/PLANT_CAVE/) | 1 Sodium |
| 1 [Star Bulb](/raw/PLANT_LUSH/), [Solanium](/raw/PLANT_HOT/) or [Cactus Flesh](/raw/PLANT_DUST/) | 2 Carbon |
| 3 [Mordite](/raw/CREATURE1/) | 1 [Faecium](/raw/PLANT_POOP/) |

One easy win: refining Di-hydrogen Jelly takes 30 Di-hydrogen, while crafting it in your inventory takes 40.

### Your next builds

| Build | Cost | From raw |
|---|---|---|
| [Save Point](/buildings/BUILDSAVE/) | 2 Metal Plating + 1 Di-hydrogen Jelly | 100 Ferrite Dust, 30 Di-hydrogen (refined) |
| [Hermetic Seal](/products/CARBON_SEAL/) | 30 Condensed Carbon | 60 Carbon |
| [Nutrient Processor](/technology/COOKER/) | 2 Metal Plating + 1 Hermetic Seal + 25 Sodium | 100 Ferrite Dust, 60 Carbon, 25 Sodium |
| [Medium Refiner](/technology/BUILD_REFINER2/) | 100 Magnetised Ferrite + 5 Di-hydrogen Jelly | 200 Ferrite Dust, 150 Di-hydrogen (refined) |

The Medium Refiner is the big one: it takes two ingredients, which unlocks much better ratios. Its first three upgrades:

- 2 Carbon + 2 Oxygen → 5 Condensed Carbon (vs 2 → 1 on its own)
- 1 Pure Ferrite + 1 Condensed Carbon → 3 Magnetised Ferrite
- 1 Di-hydrogen + 1 Carbon → 1 Faecium

The [best refiner recipes](/blog/best-no-mans-sky-refiner-recipes/) post has the rest, including the Chlorine and nanite loops.

### Your first Warp Cell

Warp Cells fuel your starship's hyperdrive, so this is your ticket to the next star system.

| Part | Recipe |
|---|---|
| [Antimatter](/products/ANTIMATTER/) | 25 Chromatic Metal + 20 Condensed Carbon |
| [Antimatter Housing](/products/AM_HOUSING/) | 30 Oxygen + 50 Ferrite Dust |
| [Warp Cell](/products/HYPERFUEL1/) | 1 Antimatter + 1 Antimatter Housing |

From raw materials, that's **50 Copper, 40 Carbon, 30 Oxygen and 50 Ferrite Dust**, using the Portable Refiner ratios above. No Copper nearby? [Our Chromatic Metal guide](/blog/how-to-make-chromatic-metal-no-mans-sky/) lists the other stellar metals.

### First cooking

Once the Nutrient Processor is built, most raw plants turn into a cooking ingredient one-for-one. The cheapest real meal is bread:

| Step | Put in | Get out |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 [Frost Crystal](/raw/PLANT_SNOW/) | 1 [Glass Grains](/food/FOOD_P_COLDFARM/) |
| 2 | 1 Glass Grains | 1 [Refined Flour](/food/FOOD_R_FLOUR/) |
| 3 | 1 Faecium | 1 [Wild Yeast](/food/FOOD_P_POOP/) |
| 4 | 1 Refined Flour + 1 Wild Yeast | 1 [Dough](/food/FOOD_R_DOUGH/) |
| 5 | 1 Dough | 1 [Bread](/food/FOOD_R_BREAD/) |

No frozen planet? [Heptaploid Wheat](/food/FOOD_P_ALL1/) or [Kelp Rice](/food/FOOD_P_WATER/) also make Refined Flour. Bread's base value in the game data is 5,200 units.

Two more worth knowing early:

- **[Smoked Meat](/food/FOOD_R_SMOKED/):** 1 [Meaty Chunks](/food/FOOD_M_MEAT/) + 1 Carbon. Base value 2,200, up from 1,000 for the raw chunks.
- **[Creature Pellets](/food/BAIT_BASIC/):** 1 Faecium + 1 plant ingredient such as Glass Grains or Cactus Nectar. It's bait: fed creatures drop Faecium and can be tamed.

The [cooking table](/cooking/) has every Nutrient Processor recipe if you want to go further.
