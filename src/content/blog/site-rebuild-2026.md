No Man's Sky Recipes has been rebuilt from the ground up. The old code had grown for years, so we started again with three goals: make it faster, easier to use and accessible to everyone.

Every recipe, item and guide is still here, at the same address. If you have a page bookmarked, it still works.

## Faster

- Catalog pages show their items straight away instead of waiting for a separate data download.
- Images are sized for your screen, so phones get smaller files.

## Easier to use

- **Item pages answer first.** The best refiner recipe for an item sits at the top, with its refiner size and time. The quickest recipe appears too, when it's faster.
- **A calculator on every item page.** Type how many you need and it works out the batches and ingredients. It handles recipes that use their own output, like Copper and Indium.
- **Recipe tables you can filter.** Search an item, then pick "Makes it", "Uses it" or "Both", and narrow it down by refiner size. On phones the table scrolls sideways.
- **Search everywhere.** Press <kbd>/</kbd> on any page, or tap the search button.
- Items now sit in their in-game colours, and long names are no longer cut off.

## Accessible

- Every type of page passed automated accessibility tests and can be used with just a keyboard. Pages are labelled for screen readers.
- Text and buttons meet contrast guidelines, and buttons are large enough to tap.
- Pages still work zoomed in or on very narrow screens.

## Found a problem?

A rebuild this big will have a few rough edges. If something looks wrong, a recipe doesn't add up, or you miss something from the old site, please [leave feedback](/feedback/). Mention the page you were on, and we'll fix it.

Thanks for using the site, Travellers.
