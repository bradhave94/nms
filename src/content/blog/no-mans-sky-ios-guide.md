No Man's Sky is coming to iPhone and iPad. Hello Games announced it on October 12, and it's not a cut-down spin-off. Here's what you need to run it, what's missing, and what to craft in your first hour.

Everything below comes from the [official announcement](https://www.nomanssky.com/2026/10/no-mans-sky-ios/), Hello Games' [iOS Deep Dive video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4IZ-uYvtH58) and the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/no-mans-sky/id1615755735), which I last checked on October 25 for price, size and system requirements.

## Release date and price

- **Pre-orders:** live now on the App Store, as part of Apple's Indie Games Week.
- **Launch:** October 28, 2026, according to Hello Games. The US and UK App Store pages both said "Expected Oct 29, 2026" when I checked on October 25. That may just be time zones, or the date may move. If you pre-order, Apple's [App Store feature](https://apps.apple.com/us/iphone/story/id6815432329) says the game downloads automatically on release.
- **Price:** the download is free and covers "the beginning of the game". The full game is one in-app purchase, Full Game Unlock, listed at $19.99 on the US store and £19.99 on the UK store.

The listing says that one purchase unlocks the full game with "10 years of updates". Nobody has said yet exactly where the free part stops, or whether future updates are included in the unlock.

## Supported iPhones and iPads

| What | Minimum device |
|---|---|
| Playing the game | iPhone 11, or iPad Air (3rd generation) |
| Planetary Settlements | iPhone 15 Pro |
| System | iOS or iPadOS 26.0 or later |
| Storage | 3.8 GB |

The listing requires iOS or iPadOS 26.0 or later, so update your phone or iPad before launch day.

That device floor is low for a game this size. The universe is generated on your device as you play, which is also why the download is so small. Hello Games' own numbers were vague (under 4GB in the announcement, just under 5GB on the Deep Dive video), but the App Store listing now says 3.8 GB. Leave a little extra room for updates.

The announcement names iPhone 15 Pro for settlements. It doesn't say which iPads support them, so I'd wait for launch before you plan a settlement on an iPad.

## What's different on iOS

Two limits, both stated plainly by Hello Games, and one gap:

1. **No multiplayer.** You're playing solo, so there's no cross-play with PC or console either.
2. **Settlements need an iPhone 15 Pro or newer.** Older supported phones run everything else.
3. **No cross-save, so far.** Hello Games' [cross-save page](https://cloud.nomanssky.com/cross-save) lists PC, PlayStation, Xbox, Switch and Mac. iOS isn't on it, and neither the announcement nor the App Store listing mentions cross-save. If you already play somewhere else, expect a new save on your phone for now.

Sean Murray, writing for Hello Games, compared it to the first Switch launch, where they listened to players and built on it later. He also said support will scale with how many people play. So these limits could change, but nothing is promised.

Hello Games also says they've done a lot of work on battery life and heat, aiming for long, comfortable sessions. I'll believe that once I've played an hour on an iPhone 11, but it's good they're saying it out loud.

## Controls

Touch controls were rebuilt from scratch. That matters in a game where you spend half your time in menus and inventory. You also get:

- Controller support
- Casting to a TV
- Mouse and keyboard, on iPad only

## What's included

It's the full game. That means over forty major updates, including [Cosmos](/blog/nms-cosmos-update/) from a few weeks ago and Voyagers from 2025, which added [Corvettes](/blog/no-mans-sky-corvette-guide/), starships you build yourself.

The Deep Dive video also confirms you can manage a Cosmos space station and play the current Expedition on your phone. Sean says the game has been a side project for nearly three years, and some of the work behind it improves every platform.
