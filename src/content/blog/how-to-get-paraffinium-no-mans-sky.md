[Paraffinium](/raw/LUSH1/) is the lush-planet resource. It's an uncommon localised earth element worth 62 units each, and the game's own text says it's "typically found on planets with a tropical environment." You'll want a stack of it for Aronium, Star Brambles and Exocraft Geobays.

## Where to find Paraffinium

The in-game description: a "local mineral extract, typically found in large deposits or extracted from common materials after inspection with an [Analysis Visor](/technology/SCANBINOC1/)."

So on any lush planet:

- **Mine the deposits.** Scan from the ground and look for Paraffinium in the resource tags, then hit the large deposits.
- **Check rocks and minerals.** Fandom notes it shows up as a secondary element in some minerals on lush worlds.
- **Use a hotspot.** Fandom lists Paraffinium hotspots on lush planets, so a mineral extractor setup there gives you a steady supply.
- **Buy it.** Some Galactic Trade Terminals stock it, per Fandom.

## Refiner recipes that make Paraffinium

No lush planet nearby? Refine it. Every recipe here has two inputs, so use a Medium Refiner or better. They come from the site's [refining data](/refining/):

| Inputs | Output | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 [Sulphurine](/products/GAS1/) + 1 [Magnetised Ferrite](/raw/LAND3/) | 3 Paraffinium | Best ratio |
| 1 Sulphurine + 1 [Pure Ferrite](/raw/LAND2/) | 2 Paraffinium | Cheaper ferrite, still good |
| 1 [Silver](/raw/ASTEROID1/) + 1 [Oxygen](/raw/OXYGEN/) | 2 Paraffinium | Uses asteroid Silver |
| 1 Sulphurine + 1 [Ferrite Dust](/raw/LAND1/) | 1 Paraffinium | |
| 2 [Pyrite](/raw/DUSTY1/) + 1 Ferrite Dust | 1 Paraffinium | Desert planet swap |
| 2 Pyrite + 1 Pure Ferrite | 1 Paraffinium | |
| 2 [Star Bulb](/raw/PLANT_LUSH/) + 1 [Salt](/raw/WATER1/) | 1 Paraffinium | |

Sulphurine is gas, so you'll need an Atmosphere Harvester or a trade terminal to bulk it up. Silver and Oxygen is the easy one if you've been mining asteroids and have a few Oxygen plants.

## What Paraffinium is used for

### Aronium, Iridesite and the Stasis Device

This is the big one. [Aronium](/products/ALLOY1/) costs 50 Paraffinium and 50 [Ionised Cobalt](/raw/CAVE2/). Aronium is one third of [Iridesite](/products/ALLOY8/), alongside Magno-gold and Grantine, and Iridesite goes into the [Stasis Device](/products/ULTRAPROD2/). Our [Stasis Device guide](/blog/how-to-craft-stasis-device-no-mans-sky/) covers the full chain.

You can also make Aronium in a refiner with three inputs: 30 Paraffinium, then either 60 [Cobalt](/raw/CAVE1/) or 30 Ionised Cobalt, then 20 [Tritium](/raw/ROCKETSUB/), 20 Silver, 10 [Gold](/raw/ASTEROID2/) or 5 Platinum. Three-input recipes need a Large Refiner or a corvette [Refiner Unit](/corvette/B_WALL_TECH0/).

Star Brambles grow Star Bulbs, and Star Bulbs feed the Fusion Ignitor and Stasis Device chains. Plant a few and you stop flying to lush planets for them.

### Refining Paraffinium into other things

- 1 Paraffinium makes 1 Ferrite Dust
- 2 Paraffinium + 1 Ferrite Dust or Pure Ferrite makes 1 [Ammonia](/raw/TOXIC1/)
- 2 Paraffinium + 1 Oxygen makes 1 Star Bulb
- 1 Paraffinium + 1 Star Bulb makes 2 Star Bulb

The Ammonia recipe is handy when you need some for an [Atmosphere Harvester](/technology/BUILDGASHARVEST/) and there's no toxic planet nearby.

### Repairs

Some damaged slots ask for it. A [Radiation Leak](/other/SHIPSLOT_DMG4/) in a starship takes 300 Paraffinium and 20 Pugneum, and [Loom Damage](/technology/WEAPSLOT_DMG6/) on a Multi-Tool takes 50 each of Pyrite, Dioxite and Paraffinium.
