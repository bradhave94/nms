Hello Games is bringing four expeditions back for the holidays, remixed into shorter runs. The NMS holiday expeditions 2026 start today with Aquarius, then [Remnant](/blog/gravitino-coil-guide-no-mans-sky/), [Our Journey Continues](/blog/nms-expedition-23-our-journey-continues/), and [The Swarm](/blog/nms-swarm-expedition-guide/), each for two weeks, through 20 January 2027. If you missed the limited rewards the first time, the [deep-sea diving suit](/other/EXPD_S15_ARMOUR/), the [heirloom set](/other/VAULT_TORSO/), the [diplodocus companion](/other/EXPD_EGG_23/), the golden [Rasamama S36](/starships/EXPD_SHIP23A/), or the [Direwasp armour](/other/SWARM_TORSO/), this is another chance.

The official post says the re-runs are remixed for a streamlined play experience during a quieter stretch of the year. Patch 7.15, which shipped with the announcement, also says Hello Games fixed mission and text issues in the remixed expeditions and rebalanced the gameplay. There is no public list of what got cut or shortened, so treat the original guides as a map, not a beat-for-beat script.

## NMS holiday expeditions 2026 schedule

| Expedition | Dates | Official headline rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aquarius (Expedition 15) | 25 November to 9 December 2026 | Deep-sea diving suit |
| Remnant (Expedition 21) | 9 to 23 December 2026 | Heirloom customisation set |
| Our Journey Continues (Expedition 23) | 23 December 2026 to 6 January 2027 | Diplodocus companion and a golden Rasamama S36 |
| Swarm (Expedition 22) | 6 to 20 January 2027 | Swarm-themed armour and equipment, plus a bonus trophy base part for the most successful team |

Aquarius is live now, as of 25 November 2026. The January dates are 2027, even though Hello Games' post leaves the year off.

The four sit in a slightly odd group. Remnant, Our Journey Continues, and Swarm all launched in 2026. Aquarius is Expedition 15 from the September 2024 fishing update, and it already had a two-week redux from 8 to 22 January 2025. Hello Games is running it again anyway, which is good news if you still don't have the diving suit.

## Aquarius (25 November to 9 December)

The holiday post frames this one as an angling adventure: follow the legend of The Angler, find a waterside spot, and equip the unique deep-sea diving suit.

Fishing itself is a permanent feature from the Aquarius update, and our [fishing guide](/blog/no-mans-sky-fishing-guide/) covers the basics. You buy the [Fishing Rig](/technology/FISHLASER/) blueprint, install it on your Multi-Tool, and cast into water. The [Exo-skiff](/technology/FISH_SKIFF/) is the deployable platform for deeper water. The expedition is the time-limited piece, with its own rewards.

The diving suit is the headline item this time around. In the game data, and on our [expeditions](/expeditions/) list, that set is the [Deep-sea Helmet](/other/EXPD_S15_HAT/), [Deep-sea Armour](/other/EXPD_S15_ARMOUR/), [Deep-sea Leggings](/other/EXPD_S15_LEGS/), and [Deep-sea Flippers](/other/EXPD_S15_BOOTS/). The original Aquarius announcement also called out a unique underwater jetpack, the [Aquarius Flight Pack](/other/EXPD_FISH_PACK/), plus the [Lost Angler's Rig](/technology/EXPD_S15_LASER/), a three-poster fishing set, and the usual banner and decal. The game data still lists a [tentacled cockpit figurine](/other/BOBBLE_OCTO/) and the title "The Fisher."

## Remnant (9 to 23 December)

Expedition 21 arrived with the Remnant update (6.2). The loop is industrial salvage: drive cross-country, strip a world of industrial waste, grab it with the [Gravitino Coil](/technology/GRAVITYGUN/), haul the load, and earn the heirloom customisation set.

The coil is a Multi-Tool gravity gun. Our [Gravitino Coil guide](/blog/gravitino-coil-guide-no-mans-sky/) covers the blueprint, which you can buy from Iteration: Eos on the Space Anomaly or at a Waste Processing Plant, and the craft: 3 [Magnetic Resonator](/products/MAGNET/), 1 [Gravitino Ball](/curiosities/GRAVBALL/), and 50 [Chromatic Metal](/raw/STELLAR2/). The [waste processing guide](/blog/nms-waste-processing-guide/) covers scrap worlds and the drop-off plants. That loop is still in the normal game. The expedition, per the official Remnant page, is the convoy event staged on a single desert planet.

The holiday post leads with the heirloom customisation set. Hello Games described it as lavish exosuit armour inspired by the traditional designs of Balaron textile merchants. In the game data that set is the [Heirloom Suit](/other/VAULT_TORSO/), [Heirloom Breastplate](/other/VAULT_ARMOUR/), [Heirloom Leggings](/other/VAULT_LEGS/), [Heirloom Gloves](/other/VAULT_GLOVES/), and [Heirloom Boots](/other/VAULT_BOOTS/). The original Remnant page also listed exclusive Colossus parts for the expedition: the [Camouflage Finish](/exocraft/TRUCK_PAINT03/), the [Asymmetrical Cabin](/exocraft/TRUCK_CAB03/), and [Mechanical Legs](/exocraft/TRUCK_WHEELS08/). Our expeditions list still shows three posters, a "TOOT TOOT!" reward, the Remnant banner and decal, and the title "Mag-field Expert."

Patch 7.15 fixed several Gravitino Coil and Colossus cargo bugs. That is worth having in place before this one starts.

## Our Journey Continues (23 December to 6 January)

This is Expedition 23, the 10th anniversary expedition. It originally launched on 16 September 2026 and ran for about six weeks. Our [Expedition 23 recap](/blog/nms-expedition-23-our-journey-continues/) has the original rewards, the redeem steps, and the milestone tables. The holiday post highlights a diplodocus companion and a golden edition of the classic Rasamama S36.

Hello Games' September post listed six exclusive rewards:

- A set of nine framed artworks, using cover art from major updates, for your base
- An astronaut bobblehead for the starship cockpit, the [Astronaut Figurine](/other/BOBBLE_ASTRO/)
- The [Starbound V0.27](/other/EXPD_GUN23/) Multi-Tool, designed from pre-release concept art
- The [Golden Rasamama S36](/starships/EXPD_SHIP23A/), a golden edition of the original launch starter ship
- A [Vintage Interceptor](/starships/EXPD_SHIP23B/), rebuilt from pre-release footage models
- A diplodocus companion, reconstructed from the original diplo models. In the game data the egg is the [Diplo Egg](/other/EXPD_EGG_23/)

The game data also lists the Time Traveller title. Our recap flags some phase mapping and a few remaining blanks as coming from Fandom, which still marks that wiki page as a stub. Use the official list above as the solid one, and the recap if you want the milestone breakdown from the first run.

## Swarm (6 to 20 January)

Expedition 22 launched with the Swarm update (6.4). Our [Swarm expedition guide](/blog/nms-swarm-expedition-guide/) is the walkthrough from the original run: the faction quiz, the Purge / Restoration / Sabotage missions, and the Hive of Glass fight.

The holiday post is blunt about the setup. The Traveller soul is fragmented, the boundaries of reality are fading, and something terrible hangs in the sky. You work with other fragments to defeat it, earn swarm-themed armour and equipment, and members of the most successful team get a bonus trophy base part.

The original Swarm page is more specific. Players are split into three teams, royal, sage, and weaver, and they work together to build the Prismatic Core in the Space Anomaly. The official reward list from that update is new posters, decals and titles, flags for your assigned soul fragment, the [Direwasp Disintegrator](/other/SWARM_GUN/) rifle, the [Direwasp Flightpack](/other/SWARM_PACK/), and the six-piece Direwasp set: [Helm](/other/SWARM_HAT/), [Chestpiece](/other/SWARM_TORSO/), [Plating](/other/SWARM_ARMOUR/), [Gloves](/other/SWARM_GLOVES/), [Cuisses](/other/SWARM_LEGS/), and [Boots](/other/SWARM_BOOTS/). The team that contributes the most "will be glorified in the Space Anomaly for all time."

That last official line is close to the holiday post's bonus trophy, but they are not worded the same. The original page talked about a commemoration in the Anomaly. The holiday post promises a trophy base part for members of the most successful team. We are not going to treat those as the same item until Hello Games says so. The game data does include a placeable [Prismatic Core](/curiosities/SWARM_TROPHY_B/), described as a constructable replica of Specialist Polo's device, and it already appears on our expeditions list as a Swarm reward. That may or may not be the bonus trophy.

[Corrupted Ichor](/raw/SWARMDUST/) is the material you pull from fallen swarmers, and it shows up early in the original walkthrough. Patch 7.15 also adds an interaction to the commemorative Weaver core already in the Space Anomaly. Hello Games didn't say, in this post, which team won the first run.

## How to start and redeem rewards

The holiday announcement doesn't give new join instructions. For the original Our Journey Continues run, Hello Games said you start from the Expedition Terminus on the Space Anomaly, or from a fresh save that you can later convert to Normal Mode. That has been the usual door into expeditions, including the January 2025 Aquarius redux.

The usual habits still help: pin the active milestone, follow the Expedition route on the galaxy map (not the path to the galactic core), and land at each rendezvous so it stays on your teleporter list.

On redeeming, the September Our Journey Continues post is still the clearest official wording we have. Exclusive rewards earned from completing expedition phases can be claimed on all save games from the [Quicksilver](/other/QUICKSILVER/) Synthesis Companion on the Space Anomaly. If you already finished one of these the first time, Hello Games has not said whether you need to run the redux again.

## Patch 7.15

The holiday post includes the 7.15 patch notes. These are the changes that matter for the re-runs, or that people have been waiting on:

- Corvette technologies could get shuffled or lost when warping to a different galaxy. That is fixed. See our [Corvette building guide](/blog/no-mans-sky-corvette-guide/) if you are still assembling one.
- Wonder records from a primary save could count toward milestones in an expedition. That is fixed.
- Objects grabbed by the Gravitino Coil could teleport to the wrong place, or fall through terrain. Both are fixed.
- Several Colossus bugs are fixed: deceased creatures stuck in the tyres, cargo flickering or vanishing in the flatbed, cargo markers on the compass, multiplayer laser rendering, and other players' cargo counting toward the valuation scale.
- You can now sit in the seats in the Vantage-Class Corvette cockpit, and wall modules can be installed there. A collision issue with the Rockhopper Wing Module is also fixed.
- AMD FSR upscaling is now supported on PC.
- An interaction was added to the commemorative Weaver core in the Space Anomaly.
- Mission and text issues in the upcoming remixed expeditions were fixed, and the gameplay was rebalanced.

There is also a pile of platform fixes (Switch Analysis Visor corruption, PS5 speed lines, Mac multiplayer, iOS crashes, iPhone Duo support, iPad resizing). Hello Games followed 7.15 with [patch 7.15.1](https://www.nomanssky.com/2026/11/holiday-expeditions-2026-7-15-1/), which is live on Steam and fixes a Steam Deck crash.
