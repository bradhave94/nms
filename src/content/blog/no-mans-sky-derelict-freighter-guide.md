Derelict freighters arrived in the Desolation update (2.6) in July 2020. Hello Games pitched them as haunted wrecks where "the lights are off, life support and gravity are offline, and the security AI has gone haywire." Six years later they're still one of the best ways to grow a freighter.

One thing first: these are not the derelict hulks from Cosmos. Hulks are huge wrecks you strip on a spacewalk. Derelict freighters are boardable missions with an airlock, logs and terminals. Our [Cosmos update post](/blog/nms-cosmos-update/) covers hulks.

## How to find a derelict freighter

**Emergency Signal Scanner.** Buy one from the Scrap Dealer on any space station, then use Tune Signal. The [Emergency Signal Scanner](/curiosities/ABAND_LOCATOR/) is single-use. The Fandom wiki lists the price as 5 million units for the first, then 10 million, then 20 million, capped at 30 million, with a daily reset.

**Free scanners.** Fandom lists Nexus missions and Iteration: Helios on the Space Anomaly as other sources. The Desolation notes say Helios offers a free one each week.

**Pulse drive.** Desolation's notes say derelicts are "occasionally detected by the starships' subspace radar while using Pulse Drive."

**Fixed spots since Cosmos.** Hello Games' Cosmos notes say abandoned freighters now spawn "at re-visitable fixed locations." Fandom adds that it's one per system and doesn't repeat. Once you finish a system's derelict, it's done.

## Pack before you board

Inside, your jetpack doesn't work, you move slowly, and life support drains. Fandom recommends:

- [Oxygen](/raw/OXYGEN/) or [Dioxite](/raw/COLD1/) for life support
- [Sodium](/raw/CATALYST1/) for hazard protection

Bring a charged weapon too. Some freighters have turrets, drones or infestations.

The airlock computer gives you three [Repair Kits](/products/REPAIRKIT/) plus a little Sodium and Oxygen. It's cold, so switch on heaters as you go.

## What's waiting inside

The enemy mix changes per freighter. Fandom lists four types:

- Minifiend nests and slime
- fixed Rogue AI turrets that wake up when you get close or shoot them
- security drones on search patterns
- slow green jellyfish that only hurt if you get too close

You can't save during the mission. You can leave and come back to finish later, as long as you haven't claimed the final reward.

## The three terminals

The route is the same every time, even though the layout is procedural:

1. **Security Credentials.** Pick these up from readable logs lying around the ship.
2. **Records Terminal.** Gives you the Crew Manifest.
3. **Secure Mainframe.** Swaps your credentials for the Captain's Log.
4. **Engineering Control.** The payout. Pick one reward.

Fandom lists the Engineering Control options as:

| Choice | What you get | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Nanites | 500 to 600 nanites | Nobody, unless you're broke |
| Fabricate Technology Upgrade | A Salvaged Fleet Unit or a Starship Reactor | Frigate fleets, or a starship fabricator project |
| Fabricate Freighter Bulkhead | A [Cargo Bulkhead](/technology/FREI_INV_TOKEN/) or Salvaged Fleet Unit | Adding freighter slots |
| Fabricate Corvette Modules | Corvette parts | Corvette builders |

Cargo Bulkheads add a slot to your freighter's cargo or tech inventory. Our [S-class freighter guide](/blog/how-to-get-s-class-freighter-no-mans-sky/) covers how many you'll want. If you're building a corvette, the module option is a free source of parts, some of them ones the Workshop doesn't sell. See the [corvette guide](/blog/no-mans-sky-corvette-guide/).

When you leave after claiming the reward, the airlock seals behind you.

## Selling the logs

Take the Crew Manifest and Captain's Log back to a space station:

- **Guild Envoy:** gives standing with the system's dominant guild.
- **Scrap Dealer:** pays in [Tainted Metal](/raw/AF_METAL/).

Tainted Metal refines into [Nanite Clusters](/other/TECHFRAG/) at 1 to 2, so the Scrap Dealer route is the nanite route. Containers on board can also hold Tainted Metal, plus [Hadal Core](/curiosities/FISHCORE/), [Larval Core](/curiosities/FIENDCORE/) and [Hypnotic Eye](/curiosities/EYEBALL/). If you're growing a [Living Ship](/blog/how-to-get-a-living-ship-no-mans-sky/), those three go straight into its organs.

## Patch 7.05 fixes

Cosmos 7.05 cleaned up derelicts a fair bit. Per the Fandom update page, it fixed freighters not being flagged as completed (which broke some expedition milestones), heaters that were always on, turrets that couldn't target the player, and drones stuck in the ceiling. Fandom also notes that since 7.05 each system's freighter and its rewards are unique, even across galaxies.
